# Refund Policy

## Damaged Products
Customers can request a refund for a damaged product within 7 calendar days of delivery.
The customer must provide evidence of the damage.

Refunds for orders delivered more than 7 calendar days ago are not eligible for automatic processing.

Refunds above BRL 500 require manual approval.

## General Refunds
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method.
Processing time depends on the payment provider.
